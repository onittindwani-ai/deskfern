# Glossary

- **Ticket:** one customer question, with its messages.
- **Source:** a place the knowledge comes from (folder, Drive, Notion, website).
- **Passage:** a piece of a page, kept with its page title and section.
- **Citation:** the passage a sentence of an answer is based on.
- **Groundedness:** whether a sentence is really supported by its cited passages.
- **Triage:** deciding to answer, ask a person, refuse or escalate.
- **Escalate:** hand the ticket to a person with a reason.
- **Approval:** a person's yes before a risky action such as a refund.
- **Draft mode:** nothing is sent or paid.
- **Kill switch:** stops every outgoing action at once.
- **Gap:** a question people ask that no page answers well.
- **Test case:** a saved question with the answer an agent accepted, used to check the desk later.
- **Held out set:** questions written before the code so they could not be tuned against.
