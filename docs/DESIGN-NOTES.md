# Design notes

- **The ticket workspace is the signature page.** The answer is never shown without its sources beside it, and the meter shows its three parts (search strength, groundedness, policy match) rather than one unexplained number.
- **Accent colour:** ochre, chosen not to match the other systems in this set.
- **Reply deadline timers** are text first (a number and a word), colour second, so they work in dark mode and for colour blind readers.
- **Warnings are quiet but never hidden:** a banner when two pages disagree, with both quotes.
- **Phone first tables** become cards below 640 pixels. Axe found no serious or critical issue in light or dark in the browser tests.
