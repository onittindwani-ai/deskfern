# Payment methods

## Cards

We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express cards. We do not accept cheques, cash or bank transfers on Team. Business annual plans can pay by bank transfer on request.

## Failed payment

If a payment fails we retry after 3 days and again after 7 days. The workspace stays usable for 14 days after the first failure, then becomes read only.
