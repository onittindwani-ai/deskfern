# Export your notes

## Single note

Open a note, choose the three dots, then Export. You can export as Markdown or PDF.

## Whole workspace

Owners and admins on Team and Business can export every note at once from Settings, then Data, then Export workspace. The file is a zip of Markdown files. Large workspaces can take up to 30 minutes and you get an email when it is ready.
