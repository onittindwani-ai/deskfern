# Status page and outage credits

## Status

Current problems and past incidents are on status.fieldnote.example.test.

## Credits

If the service is down for more than 4 hours in one calendar month, paid workspaces can ask for a credit of 10 percent of that month's fee. Ask support within 30 days. Credits are applied to the next invoice and are not paid out as cash.
