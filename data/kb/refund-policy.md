# Refund policy

## Short version

Monthly plans can be refunded within 7 days of the first payment. Annual plans can be refunded within 14 days of the first payment or of a renewal payment. Each customer can get one refund in any 12 months.

## Exceptions

A duplicate charge is always refunded in full. A charge made after a confirmed cancellation is refunded in full. Beyond these rules refunds are not given, but support can offer a credit after an outage.

## How

Refunds go back to the original card in 5 to 10 business days. A refund request is checked by a person before it is paid.
