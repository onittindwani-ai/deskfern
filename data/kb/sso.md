# Single sign on with SAML

## Availability

Single sign on with SAML is available only on the Business plan. It works with providers that support SAML 2.0.

## Setup

An owner or admin opens Settings, then Security, then Single sign on and pastes the provider's metadata. Setup takes a few minutes. Members can still use a password until the owner turns on Require SSO.
