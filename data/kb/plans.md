# Plans and pricing

## Plans

Fieldnote has three plans. Free costs nothing and allows 3 members. Team costs 8 dollars per member per month, or 80 dollars per member per year. Business costs 15 dollars per member per month, or 150 dollars per member per year.

## What differs

Version history is 7 days on Free, 90 days on Team and unlimited on Business. Single sign on is only on Business. Bulk export is on Team and Business.
