# Roles and permissions

## Roles

There are four roles: owner, admin, member and guest. Owners can change billing and delete the workspace. Admins can manage members and settings but not billing. Members can create and edit notes. Guests can only open the notes shared with them.

## Transfer

A workspace has one owner at a time. The owner can hand the role to an admin from Settings, then Members.
