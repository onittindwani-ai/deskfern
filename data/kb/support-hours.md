# Support hours and response times

## Hours

Support answers by email on weekdays from 9 to 17 US Eastern time. We do not offer phone support or live chat.

## Response times

We aim to reply within 24 hours on Free and Team and within 4 hours on Business. Urgent problems such as being locked out of a paid workspace are answered first.
