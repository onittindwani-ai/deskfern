# Invite members

## Invite by email

Owners and admins can invite people from Settings, then Members. Type an email address and choose a role. The invitation link works for 7 days. Free workspaces can have up to 3 members.

## Seats

On paid plans each member uses one seat and is billed per seat per month. Removing a member frees the seat from the next billing day.
