# Version history

## What is kept

Fieldnote saves versions of a note as you edit. How far back you can look depends on the plan: 7 days on Free, 90 days on Team and no limit on Business.

## Restore a version

Open a note, choose History, pick a version and select Restore this version. The current text is saved as a new version first.
