# Invoices and receipts

## Where

Every payment creates a receipt. Owners find receipts in Settings, then Billing, then History. You can add a company name and tax number to future invoices in the same place.

## Changing old invoices

Invoices cannot be changed after they are issued. We can issue a credit note and a new invoice on request.
