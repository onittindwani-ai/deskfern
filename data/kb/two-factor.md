# Two step sign in

## Turn on

Anyone can turn on two step sign in in Account, then Security using an authenticator app. Owners can require it for all members on Team and Business.

## Lost device

If you lose your device and your recovery codes, support can reset two step sign in after checking your identity by email. This takes up to 2 business days.
