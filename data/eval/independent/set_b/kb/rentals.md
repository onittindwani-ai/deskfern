Rentals: how they work

Where: pick up and drop off at our Boulder store only. We do not ship rentals.
Minimum: 3 nights.
Deposit: $150 card hold per rental, released within 5 business days after the gear comes back in good condition.
Late return: $25 per day per item.
Damage: repairable damage is billed at the repair cost, up to the deposit. Lost gear is billed at 70 percent of retail price.
Cancel: free up to 48 hours before pick up. After that, the first night is charged.
You must be 18 or older and show a photo ID.
