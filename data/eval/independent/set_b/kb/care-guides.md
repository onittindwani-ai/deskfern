Gear care guide
Sleeping bags: wash in a front loading machine on gentle, cold water (max 30 C or 86 F). Tumble dry low with 2 clean tennis balls.
Tents: never machine wash. Sponge clean with lukewarm water. Dry fully before storing.
Re-waterproofing: reapply a spray on treatment once a year or after 20 nights of use.
Boots: remove insoles and let them air dry. Do not put them near a heater.
