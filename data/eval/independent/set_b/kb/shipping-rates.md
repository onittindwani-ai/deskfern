Ridgeback Outfitters: Shipping FAQ
(updated Oct 2026)

Q: How much is shipping?
A: Standard shipping is $6.95 and arrives in 4 to 7 business days. Orders over $75 ship standard for free. Express is $19.95 and arrives in 2 business days.

Q: Do you ship to Alaska and Hawaii?
A: Yes, standard only. Add $12 to the shipping cost.

Q: Do you ship to PO boxes?
A: Standard only. Express cannot go to a PO box.

Q: When does my order leave?
A: Orders placed before 2 pm Mountain Time Monday to Thursday ship the same day. Orders placed Friday to Sunday ship Monday.
