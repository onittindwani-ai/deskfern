Returns at Ridgeback
(updated 2026-09-01)

- Return window: 60 days from delivery. This replaced the old 45 day window on 2026-09-01.
- Items must be unused and in original packaging, except footwear, which may be tried on indoors only.
- Used gear is accepted only if it is defective. Report the defect within 30 days of delivery.
- Refunds go to the original payment method within 7 business days after we receive the return.
- Return shipping: free for defective or wrong items, otherwise $8 deducted from the refund.
- Sale items marked FINAL SALE cannot be returned.
- Gift returns get store credit unless the buyer made the order.
