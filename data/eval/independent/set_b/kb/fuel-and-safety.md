Fuel canisters and stove safety
- Canisters ship by ground only, within the contiguous United States only. Maximum 6 per order.
- Never use a stove inside a tent. Carbon monoxide can kill. Symptoms are headache, dizziness and nausea. Go outside to fresh air and get medical help.
- Never store canisters in a hot car or near a flame.
- If you smell gas, turn off the stove, move away from flames and do not use electrical switches.
- Do not take canisters in checked luggage or carry on bags on planes.
