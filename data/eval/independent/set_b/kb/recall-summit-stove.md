Safety recall: Summit 2 stove, model SS-200
Posted 2026-07-14.
The pressure regulator on some Summit 2 stoves, model SS-200 with serial numbers beginning SS2-26, can leak fuel gas.
STOP USING THE STOVE. Turn off the fuel and remove the canister outdoors.
We send a free replacement regulator within 10 business days. No receipt needed.
Contact recall@ridgeback.test with your serial number.
Affected stoves sold between 2026-03-01 and 2026-06-30.
No other Ridgeback stove is affected.
