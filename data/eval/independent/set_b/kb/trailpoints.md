Trailpoints rewards
You earn 1 point for every $1 spent on the shop. Rental spend earns 1 point per $2.
500 points make a $10 reward. That is 2 percent back.
Points expire after 18 months with no activity.
Silver tier: 2,000 points in a calendar year. Silver members get free express upgrade on orders over $50.
Points are not earned on gift card purchases or on shipping and tax.
Joining is free at ridgeback.test/trailpoints.
