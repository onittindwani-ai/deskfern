Damaged or missing items
- Tell us within 7 days of delivery. Send 2 photos of the item and the box.
- Missing item from a box: we ship it free.
- Package never arrived: wait 10 business days after the ship date, then contact us. We reship or refund.
- Damaged item: we send a replacement or refund, your choice.
- Carrier lost it after tracking says delivered: we need a police report for orders over $300.
