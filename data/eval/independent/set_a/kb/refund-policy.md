# Refund policy
Last updated: 2026-03-01
This page replaced the earlier refund policy on 2026-03-01.

## Monthly plans
If you ask within 30 days of your first charge, we refund that first charge in full. After 30 days, monthly charges are not refundable.

## Annual plans
Annual plans can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase. After 14 days, annual charges are not refundable.

## Extra label charges
Charges for labels beyond the plan allowance are never refunded.

## How refunds are handled
Every refund must be approved by a member of the billing team. Support agents cannot issue refunds. Approved refunds go back to the original payment card within 5 to 10 business days.
