# Status and uptime
Last updated: 2026-03-10

Live status is at status.parcelpath.test.

Scale has a 99.9% monthly uptime SLA. If uptime is below 99.9% but at least 99.0%, the credit is 10% of the monthly fee. If uptime is below 99.0%, the credit is 25% of the monthly fee. Request a credit within 14 days after the end of the month. The SLA does not apply to Starter or Growth. Carrier outages do not count as Parcelpath downtime.
