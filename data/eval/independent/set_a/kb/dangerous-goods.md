# Dangerous goods
Last updated: 2026-02-02

Lithium batteries packed with equipment (UN3481) may be shipped within the United States with Northline Post Ground only. Loose lithium batteries are not accepted by any carrier on Parcelpath. Aerosols, flammable liquids and fireworks are prohibited. No dangerous goods can be shipped by air.

If a parcel is leaking, smoking or hot, move away from it and call local emergency services. Then email safety@parcelpath.test.
