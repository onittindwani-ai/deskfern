# Printing and voiding labels
Last updated: 2026-02-10

## Formats
Labels print as 4x6 inch thermal labels or as A4 PDF sheets. An A4 sheet holds 4 labels.

## Printers
Parcelpath supports Zebra ZD series thermal printers and the Dymo 550. The Dymo 450 is not supported. Dymo 550 support was added on 2026-02-10.

## Voiding a label
A label can be voided within 30 days of purchase if the carrier has not scanned it. A voided label is credited back to your label allowance within 7 business days. Voiding never returns cash.
