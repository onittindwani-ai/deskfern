# Cancelling your account
Last updated: 2026-01-20

## How to cancel
Only the account owner can cancel. Go to Settings, then Billing, then Cancel plan.

## What happens next
Your plan stays active until the end of the paid period. No refund is given for the unused part of a period, except under the refund policy. After cancellation, your data is kept for 90 days and then permanently deleted. You can export your shipments as a CSV file at any time during those 90 days.
