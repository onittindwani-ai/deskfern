# Getting started with Parcelpath
Last updated: 2026-04-15

Parcelpath is shipping label software for small online stores.

## Plans
Parcelpath has three plans. Starter costs $29 per month and includes 150 labels per month. Growth costs $89 per month and includes 1,000 labels per month. Scale costs $249 per month and includes 5,000 labels per month.

## Extra labels
Labels beyond the monthly allowance are billed at $0.12 each on Starter, $0.09 each on Growth and $0.07 each on Scale. Unused labels do not roll over to the next month.

## Free trial
Every new account gets a 14 day free trial. No payment card is needed to start the trial. Trial accounts can create up to 20 labels.
