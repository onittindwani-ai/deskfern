# Security
Last updated: 2026-02-02

## Sign in
Two factor sign in with an authenticator app is available on all plans, and an admin can require it for every team member. SMS codes are not offered. Single sign on (SAML) is available on Scale only. Passwords must have at least 12 characters.

## Data region
You choose EU (Frankfurt) or US (Virginia) when you sign up. The region cannot be changed afterwards.

## Audits
Parcelpath completed a SOC 2 Type II audit in October 2025.

## Staff
Support staff will never ask for your password or your full API key.
