# Refund policy (archived)
ARCHIVED. This page applied until 2026-02-28 and was replaced on 2026-03-01. Do not use it for new requests.

Under the old policy, the first charge of any plan, monthly or annual, could be refunded within 14 days. The old policy also allowed a partial refund of unused months on annual plans in the first 60 days. That partial refund no longer exists.
