# Carriers
Last updated: 2026-01-20

## Supported carriers
Parcelpath connects to three carriers: Northline Post, Brightway Express and Kestrel Freight. You connect a carrier by pasting its API key under Settings, then Carriers.

## International
International shipping is available with Northline Post and Brightway Express only. Kestrel Freight ships within the United States only.

## Weight limits
Northline Post accepts parcels up to 30 kg. Brightway Express accepts parcels up to 25 kg. Kestrel Freight handles pallets and parcels from 30 kg up to 1,000 kg.

## PO boxes
Brightway Express cannot deliver to PO boxes. Northline Post can deliver to PO boxes.
