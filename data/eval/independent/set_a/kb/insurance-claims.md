# Shipping insurance and claims
Last updated: 2026-02-02

## Cover
You can add cover when you buy a label. Cover costs 1.1% of the declared value, with a minimum of $0.80. The maximum declared value is $2,500 per parcel.

## Claims
A damage claim must be filed within 30 days of the delivery date. A parcel is treated as lost after 15 days without a tracking update for domestic parcels and after 30 days for international parcels. Claims are paid by the carrier's insurer, not by Parcelpath.
