# Account owner and email changes
Last updated: 2026-03-10

Only the current owner can transfer ownership. Go to Settings, then Team, and pick an existing Admin as the new owner.

Changing the account email needs a confirmation from both the old and the new address.

If the owner has left the company and cannot be reached, support can review a request with proof of company domain ownership and a signed letter from a company officer. Reviews take up to 5 business days.
